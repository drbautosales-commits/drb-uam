Vertiport Types and Requirements

Summary

Vertiports are the ground infrastructure enabling UAM operations. They range from simple landing pads to complex multi-pad facilities with passenger terminals. Design requirements depend on traffic volume, aircraft types, and location constraints. This document compares different vertiport configurations.

Comparison Table

Feature Vertipad Vertiport Vertihub
FATO Count 1 2-4 5+
Hourly Capacity 6-10 ops 20-40 ops 60+ ops
Passenger Terminal Minimal/None Small lounge Full terminal
Charging Stations 1-2 4-8 10+
Footprint 500-1000 m² 2000-5000 m² 10000+ m²
Typical Location Rooftops Urban centers Airports, suburbs

Notes

  • FATO = Final Approach and Takeoff area, minimum size depends on aircraft type.
  • Fire safety and emergency access are critical design considerations.
  • Noise mitigation may require sound barriers or restricted operating hours.
  • Integration with ground transportation (bus, metro) increases vertiport utility.
