eVTOL Configuration Comparison

Summary

eVTOL aircraft come in three main configurations: multirotor, lift+cruise, and tilt-rotor. Each design offers different trade-offs between efficiency, complexity, range, and noise. Understanding these configurations is essential for evaluating aircraft suitability for specific UAM missions.

Comparison Table

Characteristic Multirotor Lift + Cruise Tilt-Rotor
Hover Efficiency Good Moderate Good
Cruise Efficiency Poor Excellent Good
Mechanical Complexity Low Moderate High
Typical Range 30-50 km 100-250 km 80-200 km
Noise Level Higher Lower in cruise Moderate
Example Manufacturers Volocopter, EHang Lilium, Archer Joby, Wisk

Notes

  • Multirotor designs are simpler but less efficient for longer flights.
  • Lift+cruise uses separate propulsion for hover and cruise, optimizing each phase.
  • Tilt-rotor designs offer a balance but require complex transition mechanisms.
  • Battery technology improvements will significantly impact all configurations' range.
