Key UAM Manufacturers Overview

Summary

Several companies worldwide are developing eVTOL aircraft for urban air mobility. These manufacturers range from well-funded startups to established aerospace companies. This document provides an overview of leading manufacturers and their aircraft programs.

Notes

  • Most manufacturers are targeting commercial operations between 2024-2026.
  • Certification progress varies; EHang received the first type certificate in China (2023).
  • Several companies have gone public via SPAC mergers to raise capital.
  • Partnerships with airlines and ride-sharing companies are common for go-to-market strategies.
